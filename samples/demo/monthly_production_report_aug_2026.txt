CIL Subsidiary Monthly Production Report - August 2026

Mine: Suryagarh Opencast Expansion, Block A North.
Subsidiary: Central Coalfields Limited demonstration dataset.
Reporting month: August 2026.

Production target:
The August 2026 coal production target was 1.28 MT, aligned with an annualized capacity plan of 14.5 MTPA.

Actual production:
Actual coal production for August 2026 was 1.16 MT. The shortfall was 0.12 MT, or 9.4 percent below the monthly target.

Dispatch:
Total dispatch was 1.09 MT. Rail dispatch accounted for 0.74 MT and road dispatch accounted for 0.35 MT. Pithead stock increased to 0.31 MT because two rake loading windows were missed during heavy rainfall.

Overburden removal:
Overburden removal during August 2026 was 4.8 BCM against a planned 5.2 BCM. The variance was mainly due to monsoon interruptions and reduced dumper availability in the second fortnight.

Equipment utilization:
Shovel utilization averaged 81 percent. Dumper utilization averaged 76 percent. Drill availability was 88 percent and dragline availability was 72 percent.

Operational note:
The production shortfall is recoverable if September 2026 average daily output improves from 0.037 MT to 0.043 MT and overburden benches on the eastern flank are normalized.
